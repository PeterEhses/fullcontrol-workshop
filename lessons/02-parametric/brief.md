# 02 · Let the loop type it

**Day 1 · ~90 min**

## The move

A loop writes the points. Now the number of points costs nothing, and the *rule* that
generates them is the thing you're designing.

## Do

Drive the sliders first. Get a feel for what each one does before looking at the code.

Then work through the variations in the notebook — flat spiral, inward spiral, 3 points
per turn. The inward one passes through zero radius and comes out the other side; look at
what that does.

In edit mode, add a second spiral offset by half a turn.

## Leave on

Rise per turn at `0.05`, turns at `60`. The plot still looks fine.

**The layers are now 0.05 mm apart and the nozzle is 0.4 mm across. What would the
printer do with that?**
