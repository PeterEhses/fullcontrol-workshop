# 01 · The path is the object

**Day 1 · ~45 min**

## The move

There is no model and no slicer. A print is a list of places the nozzle goes, in order.
That list is the design.

## Do

Open the notebook in app mode. Change the size. Lift the corners and watch a flat square
stop being flat — nothing had to be re-modelled, re-sliced or re-anything.

Then tick **Show code**. Five points. You can see all of them.

Read the G-code underneath. It is one line per point.

## Leave on

Four corners took four lines to type. A circle needs a hundred points.

**Are you going to type them?**
